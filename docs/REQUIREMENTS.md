Functional Requirements

FR-001

Logger shall support INFO.

FR-002

Logger shall support WARN.

FR-003

Logger shall support ERROR.

FR-004

Logger shall support FATAL.

FR-005

Logger shall support TRACE.

FR-006

Logger shall support DEBUG.

FR-007

Logger shall support file rotation.

FR-008

Logger shall support Android Logcat.

FR-009

Logger shall support Hex Dump.

FR-010

Logger shall support Thread Safe.

FR-011

Logger shall support C API.

FR-012

Logger shall support Qt.
